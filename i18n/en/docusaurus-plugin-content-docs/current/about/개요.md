## Project Overview

**SceneMakerAI** is an open-source multimodal AI service that performs integrated analysis of scenes, emotions, people, and dialogue in broadcast content, and automatically repurposes this data into highlights, short-form clips, and compilations. Its goal is to help broadcast and media companies utilize their vast video archives more quickly and cost-effectively.

SceneMakerAI is being developed as a national project under the 2026 NIPA(National IT Industry Promotion Agency) **Open-Source AI and Software Development and Utilization Support Project**, and its key deliverables will be released under the [Apache 2.0 License](https://github.com/SceneMakerAI).

 ###What Problem Does It Solve?

Broadcasting and media companies possess vast video archives, including dramas, variety shows, sports, and news. However, repurposing this content into new formats—such as highlight clips, short-form videos, and personality-based compilations—is largely a manual process that requires people to watch the footage and edit it themselves, making it time-consuming and costly.

SceneMakerAI combines open-source multimodal AI—which understands both video and audio—with workflow orchestration to automate the entire process, from scene analysis to repurposing.

### Core Services

SceneMakerAI offers AI services across four key areas.

| Service | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Compilations** | Reconstructions tailored to viewing purposes, such as binge-watching guides, character-specific highlights, and emotional montages |
| **Remix & Short-Form** | Creation of short-form content such as trailers, teasers, highlight clips, memorable quote cards, and multiplatform conversions |
| **Ad Insertion Optimization** | Matching ads to scene context and optimizing insertion timing based on emotional flow |
| **Batch Automation** | Batch processing of full pipelines, short-form marketing, compilation videos with ads, and custom workflows |

 ###How Does It Work?

SceneMakerAI processes broadcast video through a pipeline consisting of **preprocessing → multimodal analysis → indexing → repurposing**.

- **Preprocessing**: Organizes signals required for analysis, such as video decoding, speech-to-text (STT) extraction and subtitling, and audio/speaker separation.
- **Multimodal Analysis**: Uses open-source multimodal AI that simultaneously understands video and audio to analyze scenes, people, dialogue, and emotions.
- **Workflow Orchestration**: Efficiently handles large volumes of content using LangGraph-based Map-Reduce parallel processing.
- **Indexing and Search**: Analysis results are indexed using vector search and a relational database, enabling quick retrieval of desired scenes during reprocessing.

Detailed architecture and technologies used are covered in a separate document.

 ###Target Performance (KPI)

The project’s performance metrics aim to be validated through ETRI-certified testing; the figures below represent the **target values** we aim to achieve.

- **Processing Performance**: Processing time for one hour of broadcast content within 20 minutes
- **Scene Analysis Accuracy**: F1 score of 0.70 or higher (for 40–60-minute segments), 0.65 or higher (for 60–120-minute segments)
