The **open-source, multimodal AI-based intelligent repurposing of broadcast content** technology currently under development as part of this project has been applied to the SBS Play “Nagoya Asian Games” AI PICK service and has been featured in IT specialty media outlets such as The Electronic Times and IT Chosun.

AI PICK is a service that uses AI to analyze video footage of South Korea’s national baseball team’s games, select key moments, and automatically compile them into highlights by inning or for the entire game.

##### Key points covered in the reports

- **Context-based scene selection**: Identifies key moments by interpreting innings, out counts, baserunner situations, and score changes together
- **Multimodal analysis**: Extracts key segments by analyzing both visual and auditory information from the video
- **Full Process Automation**: Video collection → AI analysis → Scene extraction → Content reconstruction → Service integration
- **Live Service Validation**: Validated in an actual broadcast environment; plans to expand to dramas and variety shows in the future
