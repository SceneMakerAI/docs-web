##### Paper Summary

Broadcasters must repurpose a single source video into highlights and short-form content for mobile, OTT, and social media, but the process of identifying key scenes and selecting segments from long videos still relies on manual human labor.

Rather than developing a new detection model, this paper proposes an integrated framework that connects **open-source multimodal AI analysis** and **broadcasters’ scheduling and event metadata** to the actual production process. Under this approach, AI generates clip drafts, allowing operators to focus on review and refinement.

##### Key Contributions

- **Broadcast Metadata Fusion Framework**: Utilizes program, episode, and match information as criteria for semantic chunking and as filters for segment search
- **Human-in-the-loop Review System**: Generated segments are verified against the original timeline, and operators’ edit histories are fed back as evaluation data
- **Quantitative Evaluation Protocol**: Defines an evaluation method that measures precision, recall, F1 score, and boundary errors based on tIoU

##### System Architecture

The Solbox Analysis Core generates structured scene information from video, audio, OCR, and subtitles, while SBSI handles metadata integration, segment verification, clip generation, and service integration.

1. **Multimodal Indexing**: Speaker separation, speech recognition, scene transition detection, and VLM scene descriptions
2. **Temporal Alignment and Semantic Chunking**: Aligns based on the original timeline and merges scenes based on dialogue continuity and game context
3. **Segment Search and Candidate Generation**: Generating clip candidates using vector search, metadata filters, and RAG
4. **Clip Generation and Operator Review**: Clips are automatically cut, reviewed by an operator, and then deployed to the service

For details on the open-source software used, please refer to the [Open Source Contributions] page.

##### Pilot Results

We completed a pilot by applying this system to the baseball events at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.

- **79 highlight clips** were automatically generated from the South Korean national team’s **5 games** and posted on SBS Play’s “AI PICK”
- In the initial games where the system was applied, the generated clips were reflected in the service after undergoing **only minor adjustments of approximately 10%**
- The process, which previously took about 18 minutes per clip when done manually, has been shifted to a review-focused workflow

We are currently conducting a quantitative evaluation of all 79 clips and plan to expand this technology to other sports and content such as TV dramas in the future.
