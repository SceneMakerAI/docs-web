**What are you trying to do?** I want to use an AI super-resolution model called Real-ESRGAN. Since FFmpeg scaling simply interpolates by increasing the number of pixels, the image just gets blurry when enlarged, but this model is **trained to restore** the lost contours and textures in low-quality and compressed videos, so the results are noticeably different. Here’s the pipeline:

<!--truncate-->

```text
Decoding → Distribute frames across 2 GPUs → 4x AI upscaling (640×360 → 2560×1440)
  → Downsample to 1920×1080 (super-sampling effect, cleaner image) → Re-encode using H.264 at a high bitrate
  → Merge while preserving the original audio
```
